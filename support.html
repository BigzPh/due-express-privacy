Frequently Asked Questions

⏱️ How do I create a new deadline countdown?

Tap the + button on the bottom bar to open the shipment creation sheet. Enter a title for your milestone, select your due date and exact target time, choose a color tag, and save. Your countdown will immediately start ticking.

🔔 Why did I miss a deadline notification?

Ensure notification permissions are granted: open your device Settings > Due Express > Notifications and verify that Allow Notifications and Sounds are turned on. Also make sure an iOS Focus or Do Not Disturb filter is not silencing alert banners.

🔒 Where is my deadline data stored?

Due Express is an offline-first app. All countdowns, task titles, notes, and custom preferences are stored locally in your iPhone's sandboxed storage. Your personal information is never uploaded to external servers.

📱 How do I backup or move countdowns to a new iPhone?

Because Due Express uses standard iOS local storage, your app data is automatically preserved whenever you perform a standard encrypted iCloud Backup or computer backup (via Finder or iTunes) and restore it to your new device.

✈️ Does Due Express require an active internet connection?

No! Due Express works 100% offline. You can create deadlines, check countdowns, and receive notification reminders on airplanes, underground, or anywhere without cell reception.

💡 How can I request a new feature?

We actively build features based on user feedback! Send us your suggestions at bigzapps.support@gmail.com.
