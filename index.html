Overview

Welcome to Due Express - Deadline Timer (“the App”), developed and published by Bigz Apps (“we”, “us”, or “our”). Due Express is an offline-first countdown and deadline management utility engineered to help you track critical target dates, exam schedules, project deadlines, and personal milestones with clarity and delight.

Your privacy is a core principle of our product design. This Privacy Policy details our policies regarding the collection, storage, and handling of information when you use Due Express.

2

Information We Collect

Due Express does not collect, harvest, transmit, or share any personal information.

When you use the App, we do NOT collect:

  • Personal Identifiers: Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • Account Credentials: Passwords, usernames, or social logins (no account registration is needed).
  • Location Information: GPS coordinates, coarse location, or IP tracking.
  • Device or Hardware Telemetry: Advertising Identifier (IDFA), Vendor Identifier (IDFV), or MAC address for tracking across apps.
  • App Content: The titles, dates, descriptions, categories, or notes you attach to your deadlines.
3

Local Data Storage

All information you create within Due Express is saved strictly on your local device using standard iOS sandboxed storage (such as SwiftData, CoreData, or UserDefaults).

📦 Offline Architecture: Due Express operates completely offline. Your countdown data is never synced to external servers or accessible to the developer.

If you have enabled Apple's standard system-level device backups (via iCloud Backup or encrypted computer backup through Finder/iTunes), your local app data may be included in your personal encrypted backup, which is controlled exclusively by your Apple ID and Apple’s privacy policies.

4

Device Permissions

Due Express may ask for specific system permissions solely to deliver essential app features:

  • Notifications (UserNotifications): If enabled, the App schedules local notifications to alert you when a deadline is approaching or has expired. These notifications are queued entirely by iOS on your device and are never sent through a remote push notification server. You can modify or revoke notification permissions at any time via Settings > Due Express > Notifications.
  • Sound & Haptics: Used locally to play alarm sounds and trigger tactile haptic feedback when a countdown completes or when interacting with timers.
5

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

If Due Express offers optional in-app purchases, tips, or premium features, all payment transactions are processed directly by Apple through the official StoreKit framework.

We do not collect, view, or retain your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses. All financial transactions are subject to Apple’s App Store Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

6

Third-Party Services & SDKs

Due Express does NOT integrate third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or Unity Ads), or behavioral analytics frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or AppsFlyer).

Your activity within the App is never monitored, profiled, or traded to third parties or data brokers.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored on-device:

  • You can delete individual deadlines, timers, and notes at any time directly inside the App.
  • Deleting or uninstalling Due Express from your iOS device immediately and permanently deletes all associated data stored on the device.
9

Children’s Privacy

Due Express complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations. Since the App collects no personal information whatsoever, it is completely safe for use by individuals of all ages, including children under 13.

10

Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), users have rights regarding the access, portability, and deletion of their personal data.

Because Due Express never receives, processes, or stores personal data on any server, we have no personal data to provide, disclose, or delete on our end. You possess 100% control over your data directly on your device.
